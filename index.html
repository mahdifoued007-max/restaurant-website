Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Mahdi Foued, the founder of La Bella Cucina. My passion for Italian cuisine started during my travels through Italy, where I learned the secrets of authentic pasta and pizza-making from local chefs.

Today, I bring that same passion to every dish we serve. Our restaurant is a place where tradition meets modern creativity, and every guest is treated like family.
